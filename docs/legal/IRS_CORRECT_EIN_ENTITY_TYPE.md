I am writing to correct two errors on the account for Employer Identification
Number **42-2451342**, assigned May 9, 2026 under the name **Built for Main
Street**. A copy of Notice CP575B is enclosed.

**1. Entity type**

The notice records the entity as a partnership and lists me as general partner,
with a Form 1065 due March 15, 2027.

This is incorrect. No partnership exists or has ever existed. There are no other
partners, there is no partnership agreement, and there has never been more than

entity type was entered incorrectly on the application.

The account has never been used. No returns have been filed under this EIN, no
employees have been hired, no wages have been paid, and no Forms W-2 or 1099
have been issued.

I request that the entity type be corrected to sole proprietorship and that the
**Form 1065 filing requirement be removed** from the account, so that no
partnership return is expected and no failure-to-file penalty accrues. My
business income is reported on Schedule C of my individual Form 1040.

The notice shows the street as *445 North Dohney Drive*. The correct spelling is
**445 North Doheny Drive, Beverly Hills, CA 90210**. Please update the record.
