# Letter: close the partnership EIN

Print, sign, date, mail. Certified mail with return receipt — about $5, and it is
the only proof you sent it.

**Mail to:** Internal Revenue Service, Cincinnati, OH 45999

Alternate address some IRS guidance gives: Internal Revenue Service, MS 6055,
Kansas City, MO 64108. Cincinnati is the one IRS.gov lists for closing a
business account, so send there.

**Re: Request to close business account — EIN 42-2451342**

To whom it may concern:

I am requesting that the IRS close the business account associated with
Employer Identification Number **42-2451342**, issued May 9, 2026, under the
name **Built for Main Street**. A copy of Notice CP575B is enclosed.

This EIN was issued in error. The application was submitted with the entity
type recorded as a partnership, with me listed as general partner. No
partnership was ever formed. There are no other partners, no partnership
agreement, and no partnership has ever existed. I am a single individual
operating as a sole proprietor.

The account has never been used. No returns of any kind have been filed under
this EIN, no employees have been hired, no wages have been paid, and no
business activity has been conducted under it.

I have since obtained a separate EIN under the correct entity type, sole
proprietorship, for the business I actually operate.

I am therefore requesting that:

1. The business account for EIN 42-2451342 be closed, and
2. The Form 1065 filing requirement shown on Notice CP575B, with a due date of
   March 15, 2027, be removed from the account.

Please confirm in writing once the account has been closed. I can be reached at
the phone number and email address above if any further information is needed.

Sincerely,


_______________________________
Miles Tipton
Sole Proprietor
