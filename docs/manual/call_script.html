Never call before you have run the check. The whole script is reading them something true about their own business. With no findings you are just another guy selling websites.

Goal of the call is ten booked minutes, not a sale. Nobody buys cold. They agree to look at a page about themselves.

1 · Open — twenty seconds

"Hey, is this Name? I'll be straight with you, this is a cold call. Give me twenty seconds and then tell me to get lost if you want."

Wait for them to say fine. The honesty buys the next thirty seconds and stops you sounding like a script.

2 · The observation — this is the whole call

"So before I called I looked you up the way a customer would. Typed 'best trade in town' into Google and into ChatGPT.

Google had you on page two. ChatGPT named four other shops and never mentioned you.

Then I asked ChatGPT about you by name, Business in Town. It said it doesn't have any information about that business.

You've got 21 five-star reviews, so people clearly like the work. Did you know it looked like that?"
→ Then stop talking. Do not fill the silence. The next voice is theirs.

Bad news, bad news, compliment, question. The compliment before the question is what keeps it from landing as an insult about something they are proud of.

3 · Disqualify — one question

"Quick one before I go further. Has anyone ever built you a website, or just never got around to it?"
They sayWhat it means
"Had one, it was useless"Best lead you'll get. Already paid, already let down.
"Been meaning to"Live lead. Inertia, not refusal.
"My nephew's on it"Live. It stalled at 80%. It always does.
"Don't need one, booked solid"Thank them, hang up. Don't burn twenty minutes.

4 · One discovery question — write the answer down

"Right now, when somebody who's never met you needs a gate / a bumper fixed / a couch redone, how do they usually find you?"

If the answer is "word of mouth" or "honestly, they don't," you're in. Sharper by trade: auto body — how much is insurance vs. direct? · welding, metal fab — contractors calling or homeowners? · upholstery, cabinetry — through designers or direct? · flooring, countertops — does anyone walk into the showroom who didn't already know you?

5 · The close — two times, never "when works for you"

"Here's all I'd suggest. I already wrote up what I found on one page. Let me send it over, then call you back and walk you through it in ten minutes. Costs you nothing, and if you think it's nonsense you've lost ten minutes.

You around Thursday morning, or is Friday afternoon better?"

The meeting is to see their own thing, it's ten minutes, and you gave two times instead of an open question.

Lock it

"Perfect, Thursday at nine. What's the best email to send the page to? And real quick so I'm not guessing — what kind of work do you actually want more of?"

Text inside five minutes: "Miles here, good talking to you. Thursday 9am, I'll have that page ready. 310-606-9788"

6 · Voicemail, then text — this is most of them

Most won't answer. The voicemail isn't the pitch, it's permission for the text. The text does the work.

Voicemail

"Hey Name, Miles here, I'm local over in area. I looked up Business online before I called and found something you'd probably want to know about. Nothing's wrong, nobody's in trouble. Two minutes when you get a second, 310-606-9788. I'll text you what I found."

Text, within five minutes

Miles here, just left you a voicemail. I asked ChatGPT about Business and it said it has no information about that business. You've got 21 five-star reviews so that seemed off to me. I wrote up one page on it, want me to send it over? — 310-606-9788

Specific, surprising, and a yes/no that costs them nothing. This text will out-convert the call.

7 · Walk-in — for the 52 in LA

"Are you the owner? I'm Miles, I'm local, over in area. I looked you up before I came by and found something odd. Can I show you on my phone real quick? Takes a minute."

Turn the phone around and show the check. Same flow from there. They get fifteen calls a day and roughly zero visits. Sun Valley has thirteen prospects and four auto body shops on one block of Branford Street — that's one trip.

What you're actually doing: reading somebody one true sentence about their own business that they've never heard, then asking if they knew. The sentence is "I asked ChatGPT about your business and it said it has no information about that business." It isn't about rankings or marketing. It says they don't exist as far as the thing answering is concerned, and every owner gets that instantly.
