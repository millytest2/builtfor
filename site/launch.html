The money rule, from the first dollar: business money moves through the business account. Client pays you, it lands in the business account. Never personal Venmo, Zelle or Cash App. You need money, transfer it and call it a draw.

Only three places take money to set this up: the county at lavote.gov, a newspaper service for the FBN publication, and in December the state at bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov. The EIN is free at irs.gov/ein. Anything else asking for a card to "file" is a filing mill: a .com address, a state seal, and a "state fee" above the real one.
Why the LLC waits for December 17. California's 15-day rule: an LLC formed on or after December 17 that does no business that year owes no franchise tax for it. File now and you owe $800 for 2026 and $800 for 2027. File December 17 and you owe one $800, in April 2027. Until then a sole proprietorship takes money, signs contracts and holds a bank account. The liability cap in the client agreement covers the gap.
