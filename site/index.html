What we do

Three things, in this order.

The order matters. Nobody should sell you AI before you can be found. So we don't.

First · free

Find out where you stand

We ask the assistants people actually use about your trade in your town, and we pull up your Google listing. Then we send you one page: who got named, whether you did, and exactly what is missing.

Costs nothing and you keep the page either way.
Then · the work

Get you findable

This is the unglamorous part and it is the part that matters. Most of it is filling in things nobody ever filled in.

  • Google listing claimed, verified, and actually filled out: hours, categories, service areas, photos, the services you offer
  • Your website linked on the listing, which a surprising number are missing
  • A website built so a person and a machine can both read it, with your details, services and answers marked up the way assistants expect
  • A real page for each job you want more of, naming the work and the towns you cover
  • Same name, address and phone everywhere you are listed, so nothing contradicts itself
Only then · optional

Look at where AI helps you

Once you exist, we sit down and look at how you actually work. Then we set up two or three things that give you your evening back. Examples from real shops:

  • Talk into your phone on the drive home, get a written estimate ready to send
  • Photos from a finished job turn into listing posts without you writing anything
  • Ask your own past jobs a question: what did we charge for a 16 ft gate last spring?
  • The follow-up you never get around to, drafted and waiting for you to hit send
None of this replaces a person. It replaces the paperwork you do at 9pm.
