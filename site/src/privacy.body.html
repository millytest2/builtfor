Privacy

Effective September 25, 2026

Built for Main Street is a small business in Los Angeles, California. This page explains what we collect through this website, what we do with it, and how to reach us about it. If something here is unclear, email hello@builtformainstreet.com.

What we collect

When you fill out the free check form, we receive what you type into it: your name, your shop’s name, your town, your phone number, and your email address if you choose to give it.

Like every website, the server that delivers these pages keeps a basic log of each visit: your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for, and the time. Our host keeps those logs to run and protect the site. We don’t use them to identify you.

This site sets no cookies. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking pixels. The fonts are stored on our own server, so loading a page doesn’t send anything to a font company either.

What we do with it

We use what you send us to look up your shop, run the free check, and get back to you about it. That’s the only reason we ask. We don’t sell it, rent it, or add you to a mailing list.

Who else can see it

Two kinds of companies handle it on our behalf, and only to do the job described above:

  • Our website host, currently Netlify, which delivers these pages and keeps the visit logs described above.
  • Google, whose email and spreadsheet tools we use to receive and keep your request.

We’ll share information beyond that only if the law requires it.

Seeing, fixing, or deleting your information

Email hello@builtformainstreet.com and ask. We’ll tell you what we have, correct anything that’s wrong, or delete it, within 30 days. We keep a request only as long as it’s useful for talking with you about your shop, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. We don’t track visitors across other websites, and we don’t allow anyone else to collect information about your activity over time or across other sites through this one. So the site works the same whether or not your browser sends that signal.

Children

This site is for business owners. It isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Keeping it safe

Every page is served over an encrypted connection, and form requests go straight to accounts only we can access. No method is perfect, but we take reasonable care with what you send us.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we’ll say so at the top of the page.
