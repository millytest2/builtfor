Call one: HI, SAW, ASK, BOOK

The only goal is a free 10-minute check. You are not selling today. A dial is a win. A "no" is a rep.

HIBe a person

"Hey, is this [name]? Hey [name], it's Miles. I'll be straight with you, this is a cold call. Got 30 seconds, or did I catch you in the middle of something?"

Busy? "No problem. Morning or after 4 better?"  ·  Recording? "Heads up, I record my calls so I don't miss anything. Cool?"

SAWOne thing you found, then stop talking

"So I was looking up [tree guys / a mechanic] in [town] this morning, like a customer would. Asked ChatGPT who's good around here. It named [name] and [name]. Not you. And there's no website for Google to send people to. Did you know that?"

Count to three in your head. Let them answer.

ASKOne question, let them talk

"Where's most of your work coming from right now? Word of mouth? Angi?"

"What kind of jobs do you want more of?"

BOOKA small ask

"I'm not an agency. It's just me, I'm local, and I do this one business at a time. Free check, ten minutes. I show you what people see when they look you up, and what's keeping you off the list. Tomorrow morning or after 4?"

"Cool. Best email to send it to?"

Price? "Honestly, it depends on how many services and towns you cover. After the check I give you one number, and it doesn't change."

When they push back

"Another marketing company?""No. No ads, no social media, no contract. It's me. I get you found and make it easy to call you, and you see the numbers every month."
"Word of mouth keeps me busy.""Love that. Those people still look you up before they call. Right now they find [what you saw]."
"Just send me something.""Sure. What's your email? I'll send it and call you Thursday."
"I use Angi.""Keep it if it's working. This is for people searching for you directly, so you're not paying per lead for them."
"Not interested.""All good. Is it timing, or you just don't want more of this kind of work?" Then: "Appreciate you. Have a good one."

Call two, voicemail, email

Call two shows the check and names one price. Count, then say one number.

SHOWWhat a stranger sees

"Pulled it up. Here's what a stranger sees when they look for you." Read them what ChatGPT said.

COUNTServices and towns

"How many services do you do? Which towns do most customers come from?"

PRICEOne number, then stop talking

Up to 4 services, 3 towns"$600 to build it, then $99 a month. That keeps it hosted, edited, and your Google listing right."
Up to 8 services, 6 towns (most)"$800 to build it, then $199 a month. That adds a monthly report on what Google and ChatGPT say about you, and review requests."
More, or two locations"$1,000 to build it, then $300 a month. That adds same-day follow-up on every new lead and a call with me every month."

They want less? Cut services from the site, never the price: "If we do four services, it's the $600 plan."

"Domain's in your name. Cancel with 30 days' notice. Want me to send the agreement and payment link?"

VOICEMAIL20 seconds

"Hey [name], Miles with Built for Main Street. Looked you up this morning and found something you'll want to see. It's free, takes ten minutes. [number]. Again, [number]. Talk soon."

EMAILSame day as the voicemail

Subject: quick one about [business]
Hey [name], I looked up [business] this morning the way a customer would. I asked ChatGPT for [trade] in [town], and it named other companies, not you.
I'm not an agency. It's just me, local, one business at a time. I do a free 10-minute check that shows what people see when they look you up. Want me to send it?
Miles · Built for Main Street · builtformainstreet.com · [Voice number] · [mailing address] · Reply "stop" and I won't email again.

Rules: Voice number, never your cell. No texts. Never a range. Never promise rankings, leads or revenue. Three tries, then Dead. Tap how it went after every call.

Warm: people who already know you

Same prices, same rules. Money and photos first, then the two-week clock. Never Venmo.

CLIENT #1The trainer: finish, then ask

"Hey [name], it's Miles. I want to finish your site properly. I'm putting it live this week with tap to call and your Google listing done. Can you send me 10 photos by Friday?"

When it's live, show him on the phone, then:

"Would you give me two sentences on what it was like working with me? I'll put it on my site."

"Who are two other trainers or studio owners you'd trust me with? Could you text them my number, or can I say you sent me?"

"And to keep it hosted, edited and your Google listing current, it's $99 a month. Want me to set that up?"

FAMILYMom and WeTap: a one-time fix, in writing

"Here's what $400 gets WeTap: your Google listing fixed and filled out, what ChatGPT says about you checked and corrected, and a before and after so you can see it moved. No website, no monthly. I'll send the payment link from the business account."

FRIENDReal price, a gift on top

"I want to do this right for you. How many services do you offer, and which towns do people come from?" Count.

"That's the $600 plan: $600 to build it, then $99 a month. You've fed me more times than I can count, so the first two months are on me."

"Can I use your site as an example and get two sentences from you when it's done?"

REFERRALAnyone who says "I know someone"

"That means a lot. Would you text them my number and say I'll call? I'll keep it to ten minutes and it's free to look."

Why warm first: your only sale so far came from someone who knew you. These four conversations are practice for the cold ones with nothing to lose, and each one ends with a testimonial or a name.
