The first problem: they can't find you

That isn't a ranking problem. As far as the thing answering is concerned, the business doesn't exist.

The second problem: the ones who find you give up

The hours are wrong or missing. Those are customers who already chose you and left anyway. You earned them. You just never heard the phone ring.
