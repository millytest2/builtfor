 THE CALL - one page. Everything else is in SCRIPT_FULL.txt
 Goal: a booked 10 minutes. Not a sale.

BEFORE YOU DIAL - collect THREE FACTS, not a pitch
  1. Call them twice at different times. Note 2/2, 1/2, 0/2.
  2. Open their Google Maps listing. Look for: duplicate listings,
     "CLOSED", wrong hours, no Website button.
  3. Click whatever site they have. Dead? Free Wix? Nothing?

  You are not calling to sell a website. You are calling to report
  three things that are costing them money today.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OPEN - report evidence, don't pitch
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Hey, is this [Name]? - Miles here, you don't know me, and I'll be
   out of your hair in thirty seconds.

   I called you twice yesterday, once in the morning and once around
   three. Both times it went to voicemail. I'm not complaining, I
   know you were on a job.

   But that's actually what I do for a living, so I went and checked
   a couple other things on your business and found two more. Want
   me to just tell you what they are?"

  IF THEY ANSWERED BOTH TIMES (2/2):
  "...you picked up both times, which honestly puts you ahead of
   most guys I call. So the phone's not your problem. But I checked
   two other things and those weren't as clean. Want me to tell you?"

  WHY THIS WORKS
   - It's EVIDENCE, not a claim. He can't say "not interested" to a
     fact about his own phone.
   - You're not selling yet. You're reporting. "Want me to tell you"
     costs him nothing to say yes to.
   - "That's what I do for a living" explains why a stranger checked.
   - "I found two more" is a curiosity gap. Hard to hang up on.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DISQUALIFY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Quick one - have you ever had a website, or just never got
   around to it?"

  "had one, useless"  -> best lead you'll get
  "been meaning to"   -> live lead
  "don't need one"    -> "Fair enough, good problem to have.
                          Appreciate you picking up." HANG UP.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE THREE FINDINGS - deliver as a list, then SHUT UP
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Okay so - three things.

   One, I called twice and both went to voicemail. Every one of
   those is somebody who wanted a [tree taken down] and called the
   next guy instead.

   Two, your Google listing [says CLOSED / has two entries splitting
   your reviews / has the wrong hours on it].

   Three, when I clicked through to learn more about you there was
   nowhere to go.

   Any of that news to you?"

  THEN STOP. Let him pick which one bothers him. THAT is what you
  sell him - not what you planned to sell him.

  NOTE: you are NOT promising him more customers. You're telling him
  the ones he already earned are falling through. That's a plumbing
  problem, and plumbing problems get fixed.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ONE QUESTION - write the answer down word for word
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tree:   "When everyone's out on a job, what happens to the phone?"
  Septic: "How many tanks have you pumped over the years?"
  Fence:  "How many estimates go out a month, who chases them?"
  Always: "What are you paying per lead on Thumbtack right now?"

----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE OFFER - two numbers, one breath, never the build alone
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  "It's five hundred to get you set up - the site, your Google
   listing cleaned up, click-to-call so it rings your cell, all of
   it. Then ninety-nine a month and I keep it running. First month's
   included. Cancel any time, site's yours either way. Sound fair?"

  Wants to own it outright: "Eight hundred, you own it, you're on
  your own after. Most guys go the other way. Either's fine."

----------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CLOSE THE APPOINTMENT, NOT THE SALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Let me build you a real example first. Takes me a day, costs you
   nothing, hate it and you've lost nothing. Then I'll walk you
   through it. You around Thursday morning, or is Friday better?"

  Then: "Best number? And what work do you actually WANT more of?"
  TEXT THEM IN 5 MIN. Yes = Stripe link before you hang up.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIVE OBJECTIONS YOU'LL ACTUALLY HEAR
----------------------------------------------------------------------
"How much?"
  "Five hundred, then ninety-nine a month, first month included. Let
   me show you the thing first. Thursday or Friday?"

"Don't need one / word of mouth" (you'll hear this most - 1 in 3)
  "Best customers there are, honestly. But even referrals Google you
   first to check you're real, and right now there's nothing to
   find. You lose the ones who weren't sure yet and never know it."
  -> Say it once. They repeat it, thank them and go.

"I'm booked solid"
  "Good problem. Then it's about BETTER jobs, not more. Being easy
   to find lets you be picky instead of taking whatever comes in."

"Got burned by a marketing guy"
  "Yeah - what happened? ...[LISTEN]. That's why I do it backwards:
   you see it before you pay, domain's in your name, cancel any
   month. I'd rather earn it."

"Send me info"
  "I'll do better - I'll build the actual thing. Best email? And
   Thursday or Friday to walk through it?"

----------------------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU FORGET EVERYTHING ELSE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Lead with EVIDENCE, not an offer. Three facts beat one opinion.
  Say the price out loud. Say you already built them something.
  Offer two times. Then stop talking.

  SELL THE LEAK, NOT THE WEBSITE. A website is a nice-to-have and
  they already decided against it. A missed call is a repair.

  Call 6:30-8am and 4-6pm LOCAL. 70 dials/week.
  Log every dial. Every row gets a next step and a next date.
