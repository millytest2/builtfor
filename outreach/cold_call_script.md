# Outreach — the "nothing to lose" conversation (website-first)

Not a sales script. You're a real local person who looked them up, noticed they've
got no website (or a broken one), and is offering to just *show* them — they lose
nothing. Problem-focused, chill, certain. You and Daniel, not a call center.

Tokens auto-fill from the lead CSV: `{{name}}`, `{{business}}`, `{{trade}}`,
`{{area}}`, `{{review_count}}`, `{{google_rating}}`.

---

## Tonality (read before you dial)
- **Slow down.** Cold calls fail on speed, not words.
- **Statements, not questions** at the end of sentences. Sound certain.
- **Smile on the opener** — they hear it. Use their name twice, max.
- **After you name the problem, shut up.** Let it sit.
- **Mean "you've got nothing to lose."** That's the whole frame.

---

## A. The call — no website (the wedge, most of your list)

**Open (honesty disarms):**
> "Hey, is this {{name}}? ... {{name}}, I'll be straight with you — this is kind of a
> cold call. [light] You can hang up on me if you want. Gimme 20 seconds and then you
> can tell me to get lost?"

*(let them say sure)*

**The observation (then stop talking):**
> "Appreciate it. So it's me and my buddy — we're local, we build simple sites for
> {{trade}} businesses around {{area}}. I looked you up before I called and... you don't
> really have a website. So when someone Googles '{{trade}} near me,' or even asks
> ChatGPT for a recommendation, you're basically invisible — the other guys get the call.
> Did you know it was like that, or...?"

**If "I get everything by referral":**
> "Totally — referrals are the best customers you'll ever get, I'm not knocking it. But
> even your referrals Google you before they call, just to make sure you're legit. Right
> now there's nothing there for them to find. So you're losing the ones who weren't 100%
> sure yet — and you never even know it happened."

**The offer (a step, not a pitch):**
> "Here's all I'd suggest — let me build you a quick site. Looks good on a phone,
> click-to-call so it actually rings you, shows up on Google. It's like four, maybe
> seven hundred bucks one time, not some monthly agency thing. You see it first — like
> it, we go live; don't, no hard feelings, you've lost nothing. Fair?"

**Micro-close:**
> "Cool — best email or number to send you a quick mockup? And real quick, what's the
> work you actually want more of — {{trade}} jobs like [X], or [Y]?"

## B. The call — has a presence, but it's broken (Full Visibility Fix)
For leads that already have a site/profile but weak reviews or bad info:

> "...I looked you up before I called — you've got a decent setup, but a couple things
> are quietly costing you calls. Your Google listing's got [wrong hours / missing info /
> only {{review_count}} reviews while the guys ranking above you have 200+]. That's what
> decides who gets called first. It's fixable — not glamorous, just work. I can show you
> exactly what's off in like two minutes, no charge. Worth a look?"

---

## Objections (stay relaxed — you're not attached to the outcome)
- **"How much?"** → "Four to seven hundred one time for the site, and it's built to
  actually bring you calls — not just sit there. Let me show you something first; if the
  number doesn't make sense we both walk, no problem."
- **"Is there a monthly?"** → "Only if you want us to keep it maintained — like a
  hundred, hundred-fifty a month, we check it, update it, keep your Google in sync. Totally
  optional. The site's yours either way."
- **"I'm busy / not interested."** → "No worries, I'll be quick — want me to just text you
  what I found so you can look when you've got a sec? Worst case you delete it."
- **"I already have a website."** → "Nice — is it actually bringing you calls? ... [if not]
  That's the difference. A site that sits there isn't the same as one built to make the
  phone ring. Lemme show you, two minutes, no charge."
- **"Is this AI / a scam?"** → "Ha, fair — no, it's literally me and my friend, we're
  local. I'll text you my number, you'll see we're real people."
