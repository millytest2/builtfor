  BUILT FOR MAIN STREET - THE CALL SCRIPT
  Miles - 310-606-9788 - hello@builtformainstreet.com
========================================================================

  THE GOAL OF THE CALL IS A BOOKED TEN MINUTES. NOT A SALE.

  Everything below points at one sentence:
  "You around Thursday morning, or is Friday better?"

========================================================================
  BEFORE YOU DIAL - 90 SECONDS
========================================================================

1. GOOGLE MAPS CHECK (10 sec)
   Look for the WEBSITE button on their listing.

   No button ............... DIAL IT. Use opener A.
   wixsite / godaddysites /
     business.site / facebook  DIAL IT. Use opener B. Often better -
                               they already wanted this.
   Their own domain ........ SKIP. Next row.

2. RING TEST (90 sec)
   Call their line TWICE at two different times. Hang up when it
   answers or rolls to voicemail. Write down 2/2, 1/2, or 0/2.

   About a third of calls to small trades go unanswered. If they
   missed yours, you have the only opening line in this business
   that isn't a pitch.

3. KNOW THE ONE BROKEN THING before you speak.

HOW TO SOUND
   - Slow down. Cold calls die on speed, not on words.
   - Statements, not questions. Don't go up at the end.
   - Smile on the opener. They hear it.
   - Use their name twice. Not five times.
   - After you name the problem, SHUT UP. Let it sit.
   - Mean it when you say they've got nothing to lose.

WHEN TO CALL
   6:30-8am and 4-6pm LOCAL time. Midday they're on a job.
   Work Pacific to Eastern and you get four windows, not two.


========================================================================
  MOVE 1 - OPEN
========================================================================

IF THEY MISSED YOUR TEST CALLS:

  "Hey - is this [Name]? Listen, I'll be straight with you, this is
   kind of a cold call. You can absolutely hang up on me.

   Before you do though - I actually tried you Tuesday afternoon and
   again this morning, and both times it went to voicemail. I'm not
   giving you a hard time, you were working. But can I tell you why
   I noticed? Twenty seconds and then you can tell me to get lost."

IF THEY PICKED UP BOTH TIMES:

  "Hey, is this [Name]? Straight up, this is a cold call, you can
   hang up on me.

   I'll say this though - I called you twice this week and you
   answered both times, which already puts you ahead of most guys
   I talk to. Gimme twenty seconds?"

   >> Almost nobody hangs up on "you can hang up on me."
      Giving them the exit is what keeps them on.


========================================================================
  MOVE 2 - DISQUALIFY (one question, ten seconds)
========================================================================

  "Quick one before anything else - have you ever had a website, or
   did you just never get around to it?"

  "Had one, waste of money"  -> BEST LEAD YOU'LL GET. Keep going hard.
  "Been meaning to"          -> Live lead. Inertia, not refusal.
  "That's it right there"    -> They think they have one. Opener B.
  "Booked solid, don't
     need one"               -> REAL NO. Get off the phone.

HOW TO LEAVE ON A NO:

  "Fair enough - that's a good problem to have. Appreciate you
   picking up. Take care."

  >> Do not spend 20 minutes on a no. There are 40 more names and
     your only scarce resource is dials.


========================================================================
  MOVE 3 - THE OBSERVATION, then STOP TALKING
========================================================================

OPENER A - NO WEBSITE AT ALL

  "So I looked you up before I called. You've got [forty] five-star
   reviews, twenty years in - people clearly like what you do.

   And then I went to click through and actually learn more about
   you, and there was nowhere to go. You're crushing it on Google
   Maps and you're basically invisible everywhere else. Did you know
   it looked like that, or...?"

OPENER B - A FREE WIX OR GODADDY PAGE

  "So I looked you up first. Forty-three five-star reviews, people
   love you.

   But your website's on one of those free Wix addresses - the one
   with the word 'wixsite' sitting in the middle of it. So somebody
   put real effort into building that, and then Wix went and put
   their own name on your front door. Did you know that's what
   customers are seeing?"

  If the URL still ends in /website or /mysite, add:
  "...and the address actually still ends in the word 'website'.
   That's the placeholder it ships with. Looks like it got started
   and then real work got in the way - which, honestly, fair."

OPENER C - A DUPLICATE LISTING THAT SAYS CLOSED
  (strongest opener you have - use it when you've got it)

  "Before I called I looked you up, and I found something you'll
   want to know about whether or not we ever talk again.

   You've got two listings out there, and one of them says CLOSED.
   So some of the people searching for you right now are being told
   you went out of business.

   ...Yeah. I can tell you exactly how to fix that. Takes about ten
   minutes and I'm not charging you for it."

  >> GIVE THAT FIX AWAY. FREE. It buys the rest of the call, and
     it's the most honest thing you'll do all week.


========================================================================
  MOVE 4 - ONE QUESTION. WRITE THE ANSWER DOWN WORD FOR WORD.
========================================================================

  This is the sale you make sixty days from now. Do not skip it.

  TREE      "When everybody's out on a job, what happens to the
             phone?"

  SEPTIC    "How many tanks have you pumped over the years?"
             (that's their reactivation list)

  FENCE /   "How many estimates go out in a month - and who chases
  GATES      the ones that don't come back?"

  ANYONE ON THUMBTACK OR ANGI:
  >>> "What are you paying per lead on Thumbtack right now?" <<<

  >> That last one is the most valuable question here. Most of these
     businesses are buying leads at $50-100 each, SHARED WITH TWO
     COMPETITORS. When they tell you the number, they just justified
     your price in their own words. Write it down.


========================================================================
  MOVE 5 - THE TRANSITION
========================================================================

  "Okay so - you've been doing this twenty-two years, you're five
   stars, and you're getting most of your work off word of mouth.

   Honestly that's kind of the perfect setup, because the hard
   part's already done. You're good at the work and people like you.
   You're just invisible to anybody who doesn't already know you."

  >> Never argue with word of mouth. Agree with it, then widen it.


========================================================================
  MOVE 6 - THE OFFER. TWO NUMBERS, ONE BREATH.
========================================================================

  *** NEVER SAY A WEBSITE PRICE ON ITS OWN. ***
  The second "five hundred for a website" leaves your mouth, you've
  made a one-time transaction in their head, and every monthly
  conversation after that is you arguing with your own number.

SAY THIS IN ONE BREATH. DO NOT PAUSE IN THE MIDDLE.

  "It's five hundred to get you set up - that's the site, your
   Google listing cleaned up so you show up on the map,
   click-to-call so it rings your cell, a tracking number so you can
   actually hear what it's bringing in, all of it.

   Then it's ninety-nine a month and I keep the whole thing running.
   First month's included, so nothing else comes out until day
   thirty.

   You can cancel any time, and the site's yours either way.
   Sound fair?"

  THE DEFAULT:  $500 + $99/mo    (first month included)
  IF THEY
   INSIST:      $800 once, they own it, on their own after
                (deliberately the worse deal - it exists to make
                 the first one look smart)

  RAISE TRIGGER: at client #10, new clients pay $750 + $149/mo.
                 First ten are grandfathered forever.

IF THEY ASK TO JUST BUY IT OUTRIGHT:

  "Yeah, I can do that - eight hundred, you own it, you're on your
   own after. Honestly most guys go the other way since it's cheaper
   to start and I actually keep it working. But either's fine with
   me, genuinely."

  >> "Either's fine with me" has to be TRUE when you say it.
     If it isn't, they hear it.


========================================================================
  MOVE 7 - THE CLOSE. THIS IS THE WHOLE CALL.
========================================================================

  "Here's all I'd actually suggest. Let me just build you a real
   example first. Takes me a day, costs you nothing, and if you hate
   it you've lost nothing but the ten minutes.

   Then I'll walk you through it. You around Thursday morning, or is
   Friday afternoon better?"

  Why it works: the appointment is to look at THEIR OWN THING, it's
  only TEN MINUTES, and you gave TWO SPECIFIC TIMES instead of the
  dead end that is "when works for you?"

LOCK IT DOWN BEFORE YOU HANG UP:

  "Perfect - Thursday at nine. This the best number for you?

   And real quick, so the example isn't generic - what kind of work
   do you actually WANT more of?"

TEXT THEM INSIDE FIVE MINUTES:

  "Miles here - good talking to you. Thursday 9am, I'll have that
   example ready for you. 310-606-9788"

  *** WHEN THEY SAY YES: text the Stripe link while you are still
      on the phone. Not later. Not tonight. NOW. A yes you can't
      collect in sixty seconds cools down by Tuesday. ***


========================================================================
  OBJECTIONS - ALL SIXTEEN
========================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"HOW MUCH?" (asked early, before you've shown anything)

  "Five hundred to get going, ninety-nine a month after that, and
   the first month's included. But let me show you the
   actual thing first - then the number either makes sense or it
   doesn't. Thursday or Friday?"

  WHY: Never dodge the price. Dodging makes it sound worse than it
  is. Answer flat, then move.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"THAT MONTHLY'S STEEP."

  "What are you paying Thumbtack per lead right now? ...Okay - so
   this is about one and a half leads' worth a month. Except you own it, and it
   doesn't get sent to two other guys at the same time."

  WHY: This is why you asked the Thumbtack question. You're not
  defending a price, you're comparing it to one they already hate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"IS THERE A CONTRACT?"

  "Nope. Month to month, cancel whenever you want. If I'm not
   earning it you shouldn't be paying it."

  WHY: Say it fast, no qualifiers. Hesitation reads as fine print.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"WORD OF MOUTH IS ALL I NEED."

  "Honestly? Best customers there are. I'm not knocking it.

   But here's the thing - even your referrals Google you before they
   call. They're just checking you're real. Right now there's
   nothing for them to find. So you're losing the ones who weren't a
   hundred percent sold yet, and you never even find out it
   happened."

  WHY: Agree first, completely and sincerely. Then name the leak
  they can't see. Never make them defend their referrals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I'M BOOKED SOLID."

  "That's a good problem. Then honestly it's less about MORE jobs
   and more about BETTER ones. Being easy to find lets you be picky
   instead of taking whatever comes in. And when you do hit a slow
   month, it's already sitting there working."

  WHY: If they push back a second time, they mean it. Thank them
  and go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I GOT BURNED BY A MARKETING GUY."

  "Yeah - what happened? ...[LISTEN. ALL THE WAY THROUGH.]

   That's frustrating, and honestly it's pretty common. That's
   exactly why I do it the other way round - you see the thing
   before you pay, the domain's in your name, and you can cancel
   any month. I'd rather earn it."

  WHY: This objection is a gift. The person who got burned and
  stayed on the phone is buying.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I ALREADY HAVE A WEBSITE."

  "You do - is it actually bringing you calls? ...[if no]

   Yeah, that's the difference. A site that just sits there isn't
   the same as one built to make the phone ring. Two minutes, no
   charge - let me just show you what I mean."

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"MY NEPHEW'S BUILDING ME ONE."

  "Ha - that's how half of them start, genuinely. And it usually
   gets to about eighty percent and then stalls out because he's
   busy with his own stuff, right? [laugh] No shade to him at all.
   Difference is mine's done in two weeks and somebody's actually
   maintaining it. If he gets there first, great - I mean that."

  WHY: Never insult the nephew. You'll lose. Be warm about him and
  let the timeline do the work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"JUST SEND ME SOME INFO."

  "I'll do you one better - I'll just build the actual thing. What's
   the best email? ...And Thursday or Friday to walk through it?"

  WHY: "Send me info" is a polite no. Converting it into a real
  artifact and a real time is the only way past it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I'M TOO BUSY RIGHT NOW."

  "Totally get it - that's actually why I'm asking about Thursday
   and not right now. Morning or afternoon?"

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"IS THIS AI? IS THIS A SCAM?"

  "Ha - fair question. No, it's literally me, I'm in LA. I'll text
   you my cell right now, and here's a site I built for a client.
   You can call me any time and you'll get me."

  WHY: Then ACTUALLY text the cell before you hang up. Doing the
  thing beats saying the thing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I NEED TO THINK ABOUT IT."

  "Course. What's the part you'd be thinking about - the money, or
   whether it'll actually work?"

  WHY: It's always one of those two. Get them to say which and
  you're handling the real objection instead of the polite one.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"I NEED TO TALK TO MY WIFE / MY PARTNER."

  "Makes sense. Want me to send the example over so you've got
   something to actually show her, instead of trying to describe it?
   I'll check back Thursday."

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"CAN YOU DO IT CHEAPER?"

  "I can't really go under five - that's already pretty lean for
   what's in it. What I CAN do is start you on the single-page
   version and add pages later as you need them. That work?"

  WHY: NEVER discount the monthly. Reduce the scope instead.
  Discounting the monthly tells them the monthly was made up.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
"WHAT HAPPENS IF I STOP PAYING?"

  "You keep the site and you keep the domain - it's in your name
   from day one. I send you the files and we shake hands. The only
   thing that stops is me maintaining it."

  WHY: This is your real edge. Most local shops hold the domain
  hostage. Say this part slowly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SILENCE. THEY JUST... DON'T SAY ANYTHING.

  "...Too much all at once?"

  WHY: Then WAIT. Do not fill it. Whoever talks first loses, and
  you've got nothing to lose by waiting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  >> Every answer above ends by pointing at the same place: a
     ten-minute look at something you already built for them. You
     are never closing the sale on this call. You're closing the
     appointment. That's a much easier thing to say out loud, which
     is exactly why it works.
