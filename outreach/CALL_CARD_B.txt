----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OPEN - ask a question, don't make a claim
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Hey, is this [Name]? Miles here, you don't know me - quick one
   and then I'll let you go.

   Are you still running leads through Thumbtack?"

  (He'll answer. It's a shared grievance, not a pitch.)

  "What are they charging you a lead these days?"

  THEN SHUT UP. Whatever number he says is the rest of the
  conversation - and HE said it, not you.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DISQUALIFY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Honestly - is it working out for you?"

  "it's brutal / they're killing me"  -> best lead you'll get
  "it's alright, gets me some work"   -> live, keep going
  "yeah it's working great"           -> "Good, glad it's working
                                         for you. Take care."
                                         HANG UP. Same as booked solid.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE ARITHMETIC - his numbers, not yours
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Fifty a lead - and they send that same one to what, four other
   guys? So realistically you're two, three hundred deep before one
   actually books.

   What I do is build you the version you own. Five hundred once,
   then ninety-nine a month. That's less than two leads. And nobody
   else gets it."

  DO NOT PROMISE TO REPLACE THEIR LEAD VOLUME. The claim is
  "cheaper, and you own it." Not "same number of jobs." Over-promise
  here and you own their revenue when a slow month hits.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CLOSE THE APPOINTMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Let me just build you one first. Takes a day, costs you nothing,
   hate it and you've lost nothing. Thursday morning, or is Friday
   afternoon better?"

  Then: "Best number? And what work do you actually want more of?"
  TEXT IN 5 MIN. Yes = Stripe link before you hang up.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
"I already have a website."
  "Most guys on Thumbtack do. Is it bringing you calls, or is
   Thumbtack still doing all the work? ...Right. That's the gap."

"Will this replace my Thumbtack leads?"
  "Honestly, I don't know - I'd be lying if I said yes on a first
   call. What I do know is it's ninety-nine a month instead of fifty
   a lead, and you own it. Most guys run both for a while and then
   decide."
  -> This honest answer closes better than a promise. Use it.

"How do I know it'll work?"
  "You don't yet. That's why I build it first and you look at it
   before you pay anything."

"I'm locked into a contract with them."
  "Most guys are, it's usually annual. When's it up? ...Good, then
   let's have yours live before that renews."

"How much?"
  "Five hundred to build, ninety-nine a month, first month
   included. Less than two of their leads."
